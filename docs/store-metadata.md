## Positioning

Primary v1 users are parents and athletes who want to find upcoming baton twirling events, especially contests, and explore coaches and groups by geographic region or area of expertise.

The store promise for v1 is that Twirlmate is a virtual hub for the baton twirling community: a place where community members across organizations, experience levels, and roles can explore and connect around the sport.

Approved long one-line message:

- `Discover baton twirling events, coaches, and groups across the United States`

Important guardrails for store copy:

- Do not imply the app currently supports registration.
- Do not imply the app currently supports day-of contest logistics such as schedules or results viewing.
- Do not imply the app currently supports result tracking or event management.

The first release is discovery-focused and mirrors the current app surface:

- Browse upcoming events and event details
- Discover coaches and public profiles
- Explore community groups and state-based listings

### Description

Twirlmate helps you discover upcoming baton twirling events, coaches, and groups from across all organizations in the United States.

Explore events by type (contests, camps, clinics), organization, or state. Browse coaches by area of expertise or location. Search groups by type (recreational, competitive, etc) or state.

- Event discovery with featured, monthly, and filtered views

- Group discovery with public detail pages and state-based exploration
- Deep links and dark-mode-aware UI support
